Entering Brazil should not mean navigating Brazil alone.

Brazil is a large, promising market with its own corporate, tax and regulatory logic. Foreign companies usually need to resolve all of the following before operating with confidence.

  • Corporate establishmentChoosing and registering the right legal entity.
  • Regulatory requirementsFederal, state and municipal obligations.
  • Tax and complianceA multi-layered tax system with frequent filings.
  • Banking and financeOpening accounts and registering foreign capital.
  • Customs and international tradeTrade registrations, import licences and clearance.
  • Local representationA resident representative for legal and administrative matters.
  • Licenses and government proceduresPermits that vary by activity and location.
  • Local business relationshipsReliable suppliers, partners and service providers.
